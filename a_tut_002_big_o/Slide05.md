
# 5. The Big O hierarchy

For most practical learning, remember this order:

```text
O(1)            — Constant Time
   ↓
O(log n)        — Logarithmic Time
   ↓
O(n)            — Linear Time
   ↓
O(n log n)  
   ↓
O(n²)            — Quadratic Time
   ↓
O(2ⁿ)            — Exponential Time
   ↓
O(n!)            — Factorial Time
```

Generally:

> **The further down you go, the faster the algorithm becomes expensive as `n` grows.**
